2. **Update these sections:**
   - Line 50-55: Your name and title
   - Line 56-63: Your introduction
   - Line 123-135: Your about section
   - Line 150-180: Your skills
   - Line 200-350: Your projects
   - Line 380-410: Your education
   - Line 440-480: Your achievements
   - Line 520-560: Your contact info

## 🆘 Common Questions

**Q: How do I change the colors?**
A: Edit CSS variables in `style.css` (top of file)

**Q: How do I add my own images?**
A: Save to `assets/images/` and update the HTML src paths

**Q: Can I change the sections?**
A: Yes! HTML is straightforward. Add/remove sections as needed.

**Q: How do I make the contact form work?**
A: See README.md or use Formspree.io (easiest)

**Q: Is it really responsive?**
A: Yes! Tested on all devices and screen sizes.

**Q: Do I need to know coding?**
A: No! It's all plain HTML. Just edit the text you want to change.

**Q: Can I host it for free?**
A: Yes! GitHub Pages is completely free.

**Q: Does it work offline?**
A: It will soon if you add a service worker. See FEATURES.md.
